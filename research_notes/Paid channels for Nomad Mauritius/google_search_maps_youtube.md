# Google Search ads, Maps / Local Services, Google Business Profile and YouTube for a small residential solar installer in Mauritius

Research date: 28 November 2026. Most cost benchmarks found are US data. I found no published Google Ads cost figures for Mauritius or for solar in Mauritius. Every figure below is labelled with its market and date.

## 1. Evidence of search demand for solar in Mauritius (Trends, keyword volume, scheme-driven spikes)

### Takeaway
Google is effectively the only search engine in Mauritius (about 91% share), and two policy events in 2026 should have created searches: the CEB household solar scheme (April 2026) and the Rs 75,000 grant (launched 25 July 2026). I could not get any direct search-volume data, though. Google Trends blocks automated access, and I found no published keyword volumes for Mauritius.

### Cited Findings
- Google holds 91.19% of search engine market share in Mauritius across all platforms (October 2026 snapshot). — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/mauritius)
- Mauritius had 1.27 million people and 1.01 million internet users (79.5%) in October 2025. Internet users fell 0.2% year on year, so the addressable online population is small and flat. — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Mauritius](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius)
- The CEB launched the "CEB Solar Photovoltaic Scheme (Households) 2026" on 11 April 2026, with a communiqué dated that day. It replaces the 2015 SSDG net-metering scheme and the 2021 domestic solar PV scheme, and it targets hybrid systems (PV plus battery). — [CEB news, 11.04.2026](https://ceb.mu/news/2026-04-11/launching-of-ceb-solar-photovoltaic-scheme-households-2026); [CEB scheme page](https://ceb.mu/projects/solar-photovoltaic-scheme-for-households-2026)
- The Household Rooftop Solar PV Grant Scheme was approved by Cabinet on 24 July 2026 and launched on 25 July 2026 at CEB headquarters in Curepipe. Its terms are:
  - up to Rs 75,000 per household, covering 25% of system cost
  - Rs 1.2 billion over three years
  - 14,500 households over three years, of which 3,600 are eligible in year one
  - eligible systems are a hybrid of up to 5 kW, or a 3.5 kW system without a battery
  - the buyback rate rises from Rs 4.20 to Rs 4.83 per kWh
  - panels, batteries and inverters are VAT-exempt
  - Development Bank of Mauritius loans are available for the remaining cost. — [L'Express via AllAfrica, 26.07.2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202607270203.html)
- Earlier schemes: the UNDP/GCF Home Solar Project put rooftop PV on about 2,000 households, as reported in January 2024. — [IRENA, Jan 2024](https://www.irena.org/News/articles/2024/Jan/Home-Solar-Project-Reduces-2000-Mauritian-Households-Electricity-Costs-and-Carbon-Emissions)
- An older expat.com thread (posts dated only "3 years ago", about 2023) shows the objections common before the grant:
  - one poster estimated payback at about 15 years
  - typical bills were quoted at about Rs 1,000 a month, or about Rs 2,000 with air conditioning
  - one poster said importing directly from China cost about a third of a local quote. — [expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/africa/mauritius/1017976-pv-solar-panels-purchase-and-installation-in-mauritius.html)

### Inferences
- The scheme launch in April 2026 and the grant launch in late July 2026 are the most likely search spikes of the year. Javed can confirm this himself in Google Trends: geo Mauritius, past 12 months, the terms "solar", "panneau solaire", "CEB solar scheme" and "Rs 75,000 grant". The year-one cap of 3,600 households suggests demand may be front-loaded while places remain.
- In a market of about 1 million internet users, monthly searches for commercial solar terms are probably in the low hundreds to low thousands. Google Ads Keyword Planner, which is free with an Ads account, is the only reliable way to check. This is an estimate, not a sourced figure.
- **Flag for the playbook:** the grant eligibility reported by L'Express (hybrid up to 5 kW, or 3.5 kW without battery) may not cover Nomad's 10 kW system. The fact sheet's "grant on every system" line should be checked against the CEB leaflet before any ad copy repeats it.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends data. trends.google.com blocks automated fetching (robots disallowed).
- No published keyword volumes for "solar Mauritius", "panneaux solaires Maurice" or "PV system price Mauritius".
- No data on the number of grant applications received since July 2026.
- I did not find a CEB tariff change in 2025-2026 to test against search spikes.

## 2. Cost per click and cost per lead for solar keywords

### Takeaway
US solar search clicks cost roughly $3 to $25 and leads roughly $75 to $250. Meta lead-form leads cost roughly a third of that, but their quality is much lower. No Mauritius-specific CPC data is published. Mauritian CPCs are very likely far lower than US figures, because fewer advertisers compete for each auction, but that is an inference.

### Cited Findings
- **US solar Google search CPCs (late 2024 to 2025, LeadGen Economy, marked directional):**
  - "solar quotes": $8–15
  - "solar installers near me": $10–18
  - "solar panel cost": $5–10
  - "how much is solar": $3–6
  - Conversion rates by keyword run from 2–4% up to 7–10%. — [LeadGen Economy, Nov 2025, updated Mar 2026](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/google-vs-facebook-solar-leads-comparison/)
- **US solar CPL (same source):**
  - Exclusive Google leads: $80–220
  - Effective CPL by keyword: $75–250
  - Facebook Lead Ads (native form): $25–55
  - Facebook to a landing page: $45–90 — [LeadGen Economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/google-vs-facebook-solar-leads-comparison/)
- **WordStream 2025 benchmarks, US, Apr 2024–Mar 2025 (16,446 campaigns, medians):**
  - Home & Home Improvement: CPC $7.85, CTR 6.37%, conversion rate 7.33%, CPL $90.92
  - All industries: CPC $5.26, CPL $70.11 — [WordStream 2025 Google Ads Benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks)
- Mauritian Google Ads agencies plan budgets in MUR but publish no CPC or budget figures. — [Korhogo Agency](https://korhogoagency.com/google-ads-mauritius/)

### Inferences
- A US CPC of $5–15 is a ceiling, not an estimate, for Mauritius. Mauritian solar auctions likely have only a handful of bidders, which usually means CPCs well under $1–2. Keyword Planner's "top of page bid" range for MU will give real figures.
- At an assumed Mauritian CPC of $0.50–1.50 and a 5–8% conversion rate (in line with US benchmarks), one lead would cost roughly $6–30. This is illustrative arithmetic only; none of these inputs are measured.

### Gaps
- No Mauritius or Indian Ocean (Réunion, Seychelles) CPC data for any industry was found.
- No data from small emerging markets on solar CPL specifically.

## 3. Minimum practical Google Ads budget; is $3–10 a day viable

### Takeaway
Google Ads has no formal minimum spend, but $3–10 a day buys only a few clicks a day even at low CPCs. That is enough for a narrow, exact-intent campaign. It is not enough to let automated bidding learn.

### Cited Findings
- Agency guides on 2026 Google Ads budgets and daily minimums exist but are US-centred. — [Newframe Digital](https://newframedigital.com/google-ads-budget-how-much-to-spend/); [Hire Ads Guru](https://www.hireadsguru.com/google-ads-daily-budget-for-small-business-how-much-should-you-spend/). I did not read either in full, so their specific figures are not cited here.
- Quality Score improvements (5 to 7) cut CPC by about 20%, and negative keyword lists prevent an estimated 15–25% of wasted clicks. — [LeadGen Economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/google-vs-facebook-solar-leads-comparison/)

### Inferences
- $5 a day is about $150 a month. If Mauritian CPC is about $1, that is about 150 clicks and perhaps 5–12 enquiries a month at US-like conversion rates. That would be meaningful against a capacity of about 4 installs a month, but it is unverified.
- At this budget the practical setup is:
  - manual CPC or Maximise Clicks with a bid cap
  - exact and phrase match only, in English and French
  - strong negatives: "job", "kit", "Alibaba", "DIY", "free", "battery for car" and similar
  - the call or WhatsApp click as the conversion
  - geo set to Mauritius only
  - a schedule limited to working hours
- Smart bidding such as Maximise Conversions usually needs conversion volume, which a tiny account won't generate quickly. This is general practitioner knowledge, not sourced here.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative Google help-doc statement on a minimum budget. The rule that a daily budget can be set to any amount, with overspend up to 2x on a single day, is from general knowledge and needs verifying in Google Ads Help.

## 5. Google Business Profile and reviews; Local Services Ads in Mauritius

### Takeaway
Reviews matter a great deal, and consumers increasingly filter by rating and recency. This supports the playbook's "review at every install" rule. Local Services Ads almost certainly cannot be used in Mauritius.

### Cited Findings
- **BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (survey market not stated on the page; historically US):**
  - 71% use Google to evaluate local businesses (83% in 2025)
  - 97% read reviews
  - 68% use only businesses rated 4 stars or more (55% in 2025)
  - 31% require 4.5 stars or more (17% in 2025)
  - 74% look for reviews from the last three months
  - 89% expect owners to respond
  - 32% expect a response by the next day — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)
- Google's Local Services Ads help page lists only these countries in its selector: Austria, Belgium, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Spain, Switzerland, the UK and the US. Mauritius and other African countries are absent. That selector may be a list of language versions rather than a definitive availability list. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/adwords/answer/6224841)
- Local Services Ads expanded to Europe in 2020, but only to a set of Western European markets. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-takes-local-services-ads-to-europe-341284)

### Inferences
- Local Services Ads are not available in Mauritius. This is very likely, but rests on the absence of Mauritius from Google's lists. The substitute is a well-kept, free Google Business Profile, plus optional location assets in Search ads, which show the Maps pin, address and call button.
- A free profile with 20+ recent reviews is probably the highest-return Google asset for Nomad before any paid spend. It matches the playbook's June 2027 target of 20+ reviews averaging 4.8 or more.

### Gaps
- No Mauritius-specific consumer review survey was found.
- Not confirmed whether Google Maps "promoted pins" (location ads via Performance Max) serve in Mauritius.

## 6. YouTube ads in Mauritius: reach, cost, usefulness for explainers

### Takeaway
YouTube views are cheap globally (about $0.05 per in-stream view; CPM $5–10), and costs in a small market are likely lower. YouTube builds awareness, though, not intent. For Nomad it is mainly a cheap way to put Javed's explainer videos in front of Mauritian homeowners.

### Cited Findings
- **Global YouTube benchmarks (Store Growers, updated 19 Feb 2026):**
  - CPM: average $5–10; Shorts about $4; bumpers $3.24–4.37
  - CPV: in-stream about $0.05; skippable $0.05–0.10
  - CTR: about 0.65% overall; in-feed 1–3%
  - The source gives no African country data. — [Store Growers](https://www.storegrowers.com/youtube-ads-benchmarks/)
- **DataReportal Digital 2026: Mauritius (late 2025):** Facebook ad reach 886,000 (69.9% of population), Instagram 418,000 (33.0%). No YouTube or TikTok ad reach figure is shown on the page. — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius)
- LinkedIn had 570,000 registered members in Mauritius in early 2025 (registered accounts, not active users). — [DataReportal 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-mauritius)

### Inferences
- Meta reaches about 70% of the population, and YouTube's Mauritian reach is unverified. Meta remains the primary paid reach channel. YouTube could serve as a cheap retargeting or explainer layer, with the same 40-second videos uploaded and promoted as in-feed or skippable ads to Mauritius 25–65 homeowners.
- Usefulness is limited by lead path: YouTube CTRs are under 1%, so few viewers will act directly. Its value is familiarity before a search or a WhatsApp message.

### Gaps
- No YouTube ad reach or CPM/CPV figure for Mauritius was found. Javed can see estimated reach in the Google Ads campaign planner.

## 7. Search vs social: lead quality and close rates for residential solar

### Takeaway
US industry data consistently shows search leads cost more but contact, qualify and close at roughly two times the rate of Meta lead-form leads. As a result, cost per sale can be lower on Google ($1,300 vs $2,500 in one worked example). Note that Nomad's Meta plan sends people to WhatsApp, not to a lead form, which narrows the gap.

### Cited Findings
- **LeadGen Economy (US, late 2024 to 2025, directional):**
  - Valid phone: Google 90–95%, Facebook Lead Ads 70–80%
  - First-attempt contact: Google 55–70%, Facebook Lead Ads 25–40%
  - Meets basic qualification: Google 60–75%, Facebook Lead Ads 30–45%
  - Converts to sale: Google 8–15%, Facebook Lead Ads 3–6%, Facebook to a landing page 5–9% — [LeadGen Economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/google-vs-facebook-solar-leads-comparison/)
- **Worked example from the same source (US Tier-2 market):**
  - CPL: Google $130, Facebook $45
  - Cost per qualified lead: $363 vs $428
  - Close rate: 10% vs 6%
  - Cost per sale: $1,300 vs $2,500 — [LeadGen Economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/google-vs-facebook-solar-leads-comparison/)
- In the same source, Facebook landing-page leads cost 40–60% more than Lead Ads but are 50–80% better quality. Qualifying questions cut volume 15–25% and improve quality 30–40%. — [LeadGen Economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/google-vs-facebook-solar-leads-comparison/)
- Caveat on the source: LeadGen Economy is a lead-generation industry blog. It says its figures are directional and does not attribute them to its listed sources (SEIA, EnergySage and others).

### Inferences
- For Nomad, the "send a photo of your CEB bill" step is itself a strong qualifier on any channel. Search's quality edge may be smaller than in US lead-form comparisons, because both channels end in a WhatsApp conversation.
- A sensible test, consistent with the playbook's rule of no paid media until shipment 1 is installed, is a 4–6 week Search test at about $5 a day alongside the Meta boost budget. Use exact-intent English and French terms, and track bills received per channel in the lead sheet's Source column. Run the free Keyword Planner and Transparency Center checks first. If monthly search volume turns out to be tiny, the test is not worth running.

### Gaps
- No independent close-rate data from EnergySage, SolarReviews or a similar first-party source was found in this pass.
- No emerging-market comparison of search vs social lead quality was found.
