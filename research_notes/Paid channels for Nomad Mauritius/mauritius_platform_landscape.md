# Mauritius social media and digital platform landscape (for advertising residential solar to homeowners)

Research date: 28 Nov 2026. Main sources: DataReportal (Kepios/Meltwater) Digital 2025 and Digital 2026 Mauritius; NapoleonCat (Meta ad-tool based, Oct 2026); Statistics Mauritius ICT data via L'Express (July 2026); StatCounter (Oct 2026).

## 1. Ad-reachable audience and penetration by platform

### Takeaway
Facebook is by far the largest reachable platform in Mauritius: about 886,000 ad reach (Oct 2025), 85.5% of adults 18+ and 88% of internet users. Messenger reaches about 600,000 and Instagram about 418,000. LinkedIn's 630,000 is registered members, not active users. DataReportal publishes no Mauritius figures for TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat or Pinterest.

### Cited Findings
**Digital 2026: Mauritius (data mostly October 2025)** — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Mauritius](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius)
- Population 1.27 million; median age 37.8; 41.1% urban / 58.9% rural.
- Age structure (share of total population): 25-34 15.8%, 35-44 15.0%, 45-54 14.0%, 55-64 12.8%, 65+ 14.1%. So ages 35-64 make up 41.8%, about 530,000 people.
- Social media user identities: 886,000 (69.9% of population; 85.5% of adults 18+; 88.0% of internet users). Up 45,000 (+5.3%) year on year.
- **Facebook ad reach: 886,000.** That is 69.9% of the population, 79.8% of people aged 13+, 85.5% of adults and 88.0% of internet users. Up 44,600 (+5.3%) from Oct 2024 to Oct 2025.
- **Instagram ad reach: 418,000.** That is 33.0% of the population, 40.0% of adults and 41.5% of internet users. Up 35,400 (+9.2%) year on year.
- **Messenger ad reach: 599,000.** That is 47.2% of the population and 57.6% of adults. Up 10,400 (+1.8%).
- **LinkedIn registered members: 630,000.** That is 49.7% of the population and 60.8% of adults. Up 90,000 (+16.7%). These are total registered members, not active users.
- **X ad reach: 210,000** (16.6% of the population), up 105% in a year. DataReportal flags X's figures as unusual fluctuations and advises caution.
- Reddit: 159,000 (12.5%), also with unusual fluctuations.
- No figures are given for TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat or Pinterest.
- Caveats from DataReportal: Meta ad reach "is not a proxy for monthly or daily active users". Meta revised its audience data, so the figures are not comparable with earlier reports. User identities may not be unique people.

**Digital 2025: Mauritius (early 2025)** — [DataReportal Digital 2025: Mauritius](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-mauritius)
- Facebook 859,000 (67.7% of population; 82.7% of adults 18+); Instagram 399,000 (31.4%); Messenger 599,000 (47.2%); LinkedIn 570,000 members; X 77,100.
- Again, no TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat or Pinterest data.

**Digital 2024 (early 2024)** — [DataReportal Digital 2024: Mauritius](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-mauritius)
- Facebook 820,900; Instagram 390,500. No TikTok or YouTube figures either.

**NapoleonCat (Meta ad-tool based), October 2026** — [NapoleonCat Mauritius social media users](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/social-media-users-in-mauritius/2025); [NapoleonCat Facebook users in Mauritius](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-mauritius/2025/05). The 2025 URLs redirected to the latest month, October 2026.
- Facebook 1,190,100 (stated as 90% of population); Instagram 511,200 (38.7%); Messenger 989,800 (74.9%); LinkedIn 572,000 (43.3%).
- **These conflict with DataReportal.** NapoleonCat's Facebook figure is about 34% higher, and its Messenger figure about 65% higher. NapoleonCat reports Meta's ad-audience estimates without adjustment, and these usually include visitors, members of the diaspora tagged to Mauritius, and duplicate accounts. Treat NapoleonCat as an upper bound and DataReportal (about 886,000) as the conservative figure.

### Inferences
- Adults aged 18 and over number about 1.04 million (886k ÷ 85.5%). Facebook reaches most of them. No other platform reaches more than about 60% of adults.
- TikTok and YouTube reach cannot be sized from public data. Their absence from DataReportal's Mauritius report suggests TikTok does not expose a Mauritius ad audience. That is not confirmed (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No public TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat or Pinterest reach figures for Mauritius were found from any source.
- I could not confirm whether TikTok Ads Manager can deliver ads to Mauritius. [TikTok's location help page](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/placements-available-locations?lang=en) shows the list only through an interactive dropdown. The way to check is to open Ads Manager and try Mauritius as a target location.
- I found no Google Ads (YouTube) audience figures for Mauritius quoted anywhere.

## 2. Age and gender split by platform (focus: adults 30-65)

### Takeaway
Meta audiences in Mauritius are close to gender-balanced, with Facebook about 52% male and 46% female. The 25-34 bracket is the largest single group, but ages 35-64 still account for about 45% of Facebook users. So Nomad's audience of 85-90% male, aged 35-54, comes from its own targeting and creative, or from who engages with solar. It does not reflect the platform's makeup.

### Cited Findings
- Facebook, adults: 46.2% female / 52.1% male (Oct 2025) — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius). Early 2025: 47.1% / 52.9% — [DataReportal 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-mauritius).
- Instagram: 49.2% female / 49.1% male (Oct 2025) — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius).
- Messenger: 47.5% female / 52.0% male — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius).
- LinkedIn: 48.8% female / 51.2% male — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius).
- X: 19.0% female / 80.9% male, inferred from profile names, so low reliability — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius).
- Facebook by age, NapoleonCat Oct 2026, as a share of users: 18-24 16.7%, 25-34 30.1%, 35-44 19.7%, 45-54 14.7%, 55-64 10.4%, 65+ 8.4%. Under-18 audiences are under 1,000 — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-mauritius/2025/05).
  - The male/female split within each age group is not labelled on the page.
  - The page's statement that men lead women by 194,300 in the 25-34 group is internally inconsistent with the overall 51.8/48.2 split, so do not rely on it.
  - Overall split on that page: 51.8% men / 48.2% women.
- Instagram's largest age group is 25-34 (about 192,000); Messenger's is 25-34 (283,600) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/social-media-users-in-mauritius/2025).
- LinkedIn by age (NapoleonCat): 18-24 29.7%, 25-34 49%, 35-54 19.2%, 55+ 2.1%. That is very young, with few older members — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/social-media-users-in-mauritius/2025).

### Inferences
- Applying NapoleonCat's age shares to DataReportal's 886,000 gives about 400,000 Facebook users aged 35-64, and about 300,000 aged 35-54. Applied to NapoleonCat's own 1.19 million, the 35-64 figure is about 535,000.
- An ad set for ages 35-54 in Mauritius therefore has a pool of about 300,000 to 410,000. That is far larger than a $3/day budget can saturate.
- LinkedIn is a weak channel for homeowners aged 45-65: only 2.1% of its members are 55+, and about 19% are 35-54.
- The 85-90% male skew Nomad sees is probably a mix of three things: who makes the household energy decision, who engages with technical content, and how Meta's delivery optimises toward early responders. It does not reflect the platform. Female co-decision-makers are under-reached.

### Gaps
- No reliable age-by-gender tables for Mauritius were found. NapoleonCat's per-age gender columns are unlabelled.
- No public data links home ownership to platform use.

## 3. WhatsApp usage in Mauritius

### Takeaway
No platform-specific WhatsApp penetration figure for Mauritius was found. DataReportal does not report WhatsApp ad reach, because WhatsApp has no ad inventory. Statistics Mauritius shows that 96.5% of internet users made internet calls in 2024. That is indirect but strong evidence of near-universal use of messaging and calling apps.

### Cited Findings
- 96.5% of internet users made internet calls in 2024, up from 67.4% in 2020. 89.1% used social networks — Statistics Mauritius via [L'Express/AllAfrica, July 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202607130229.html).
- Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform charges per message for marketing templates sent outside the free customer-service window. India's rate effective 1 Oct 2026 is 0.8631 INR per marketing message — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks).
- Messages started by the customer through click-to-WhatsApp ads open a free service window: [Meta WhatsApp pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) (not fetched; cited by Spur).

### Inferences
- Internet calling is near-universal, and Messenger is comparatively weak (about 58% of adults on DataReportal). Together these suggest WhatsApp is the dominant messaging app, consistent with Nomad's experience. This is an inference, not a measured figure.

### Gaps
- No measured WhatsApp penetration for Mauritius was found: not from DataReportal, Statistics Mauritius, ICTA, or the local press.
- No local study on business use of WhatsApp was found.

## 4. Internet, smartphones, search share, time online

### Takeaway
Mauritius is highly connected. About 80% of the population uses the internet (83% of those aged 12+). 88.6% of households own a smartphone. Google holds 91% of search. Internet use among people aged 60+ doubled from 26% to 53% between 2020 and 2024.

### Cited Findings
- Internet users: 1.01 million, 79.5% penetration; 260,000 offline (late 2025). 2.25 million mobile connections, 178% of the population; 97.1% of connections are 3G/4G/5G. Median fixed download speed 57.08 Mbps — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mauritius).
- Statistics Mauritius, 2024 (published 10 July 2026) — [L'Express via AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202607130229.html):
  - Household access: 88.6% of households have a smartphone (81.4% in 2020); 85.8% have home internet (72.6% in 2020).
  - Individuals: 93% of people aged 5+ use a mobile phone; 83.2% of people aged 12+ use the internet (68.3% in 2020).
  - By age: 97.5% of 12-19s and 98.6% of 20-29s use the internet. Among people 60+, internet use is 53.0% (26.2% in 2020), while only 12.6% use a computer.
  - Online activity: 36.1% of internet users bank online; 25.4% of the population shops online.
- 2024 internet subscriptions: 2,216,400, of which 83.9% are mobile broadband; 178.1 per 100 inhabitants — [L'Express via AllAfrica, 2025](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202507180451.html).
- Search engine share, Oct 2026: Google 91.19%, Bing 7.50%, Yahoo 0.46% — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/mauritius).

### Inferences
- Older homeowners (60+) are reachable, but they use phones rather than computers. Ads and landing pages must work on mobile first.
- With Google at 91% of search, search ads are a realistic second channel for people with clear intent ("solar panels Mauritius"). Expect small volumes in a market of 1.27 million people.

### Gaps
- No Mauritius-specific data on daily time spent online or on social media was found. DataReportal's Mauritius pages do not report it.

## 7. Meta ad costs (CPM, CPC, cost per message)

### Takeaway
No published, reliable Mauritius CPM, CPC or cost-per-message benchmark was found. Public sources say none exist for click-to-WhatsApp. Nomad's own figure of about $0.60 per conversation is the best benchmark available. One sizeable European vendor range is €1.50 to €8.00 per conversation, which puts Nomad's cost very low by comparison.

### Cited Findings
- Spur (checked 29 Sep to 1 Oct 2026) — [Spur click-to-WhatsApp benchmarks](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks):
  - It states there are no independent public benchmarks for click-to-WhatsApp CTR or cost per conversation, and it advises building a 30-day baseline in-market.
  - A vendor guide (Chatarmin) puts the cost per conversation at €1.50 to €8.00 by industry, with no stated sample, in mainly European markets.
  - Meta's own test across 734,000 businesses: ads with more than one message destination had a 9.5% lower median cost per message (H2 2024).
- An Indian real-estate case ran into Instagram DMs, not WhatsApp. Its cost per message start was about ₹33-36 (about $0.40) — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks).
- Superads publishes country median CPMs from more than $3B of aggregated spend. Its Mauritius page (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026) loaded with no values in the fetched content — [Superads Mauritius CPM](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-mille/mauritius).

### Inferences
- Nomad's figures are about 1 conversation per 115-135 people reached, and about $0.60 per conversation, at $3/day.
- That implies roughly $0.0045-0.005 per person reached, or about $4.50-5.20 per 1,000 reach. This is an approximation of CPM, since reach is not impressions. It is plausible for a small market.
- About 5 conversations a day at $3/day is well below a ~300k+ audience pool, so audience saturation is not a near-term constraint. Frequency and creative fatigue are.

### Gaps
- No numeric Mauritius CPM, CPC or cost-per-message figure from a verifiable source.
- Superads' figures may display in a browser (the page appears script-rendered). The values were not visible to this fetch.

## Notes on source conflicts
- Facebook reach: DataReportal gives 886k (Oct 2025) and NapoleonCat 1.19m (Oct 2026). Messenger: 599k versus 990k.
- The difference is probably methodology: DataReportal adjusts Meta's figures, and NapoleonCat reports the raw ad-tool output. A one-year gap and Meta's audience revision add to it. Use about 0.9 to 1.2 million as the range.
- DataReportal's reports date-stamp data from the prior autumn: "Digital 2026" is October 2025 data.
